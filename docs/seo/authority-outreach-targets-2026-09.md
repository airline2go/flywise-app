# Airpiv Authority Outreach Targets — September 2026

## Goal
Earn editorial, research, data-citation, and resource links to Airpiv's public flight-data assets. This document is an execution list, not a promise that any target will link.

## Primary Airpiv assets

1. `/research/flight-data` — primary data-index citation target.
2. Route-network research asset — secondary route/network citation target.
3. Germany airport-connectivity research asset — secondary German-market target.

## Outreach sequencing

### Wave 1 — data institutions
Contact the public/open-data/data-publication teams first. The ask should be narrow: cite Airpiv only where it answers a concrete user question that the existing resource does not address, such as a public route catalogue or route-coverage index.

### Wave 2 — aviation research
Approach research groups, aviation data publications, and university projects with a specific figure/table/methodology use case. Offer a stable URL and a short description of what is measured and what is deliberately not measured.

### Wave 3 — airport and destination resources
Approach airport, regional, and destination information resources only where a route-specific or connectivity-specific reference materially improves their page. Link to the relevant Airpiv route page or research asset, not to a generic homepage.

## Link-placement rules

- Prefer contextual editorial links over directory/profile links.
- Use branded or topic-relevant anchors such as `Airpiv flight route data`, `flight route index`, `Flugstreckendaten`, or `Flugverbindungen` rather than repeating exact-match commercial phrases.
- A route-page link should be used only when the source page is specifically about that route.
- Do not request reciprocal links as a condition of inclusion.
- Do not use automated backlink services, bulk directory submissions, or paid links intended to pass ranking value.

## Evidence package to attach to outreach

1. Stable Airpiv research URL.
2. One-sentence methodology summary.
3. Snapshot date / freshness indicator.
4. Short explanation of the measured fields.
5. Clear distinction between route-catalogue coverage and traveller demand.
6. Optional CSV/download reference when the asset exposes one.

## Success metrics

Track referring domains, link placement quality, destination URL, anchor text, publication date, indexed status, GSC impressions/clicks/average position for linked route pages, and citations/mentions of the Airpiv data assets.

Third-party authority scores are diagnostics only; they are not Google ranking metrics.
